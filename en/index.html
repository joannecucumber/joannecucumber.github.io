About Me

After nearly 10 years of building products, I now lead a 30+ person design team. The current focus is redefining the designer’s role and how teams should work in the age of AI. I love exploring new things, thinking deeply, and using design to solve problems.

  • As a Product Designer

    Nearly 10 years in product design, shipping 20+ fintech and lifestyle apps across 0-to-1 launches, growth, subscriptions and major redesigns. I turn research and fuzzy problems into actionable product decisions.

    Lead a 30+ person design department, aligning direction and capability expectations, supporting professional judgment, and turning AI exploration into shared methods that help designers work more independently.
