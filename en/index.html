About Me

After nearly 10 years of building products, I now lead a 30+ person design team. Like the rest of the world, I’m redefining the designer’s role and how teams should work in the age of AI. I love exploring new things, thinking deeply, and using design to solve problems.

  • As a Product Designer

    Nearly 10 years in product design, shipping 20+ fintech and lifestyle apps across 0-to-1 launches, growth, subscriptions and major redesigns. I turn research and fuzzy problems into actionable product decisions.

  • As a Design Lead

    I lead a 30+ person design department, building standards, reviews and talent development that raise the bar from polished visuals to sound product judgment. I also lead AI x Design Ops, cutting workflow time by 60–90%.

  • As Myself

    Simply put, I can't sit still. I love nature, and I also love being a homebody nerd digging into new things. This year I got a dog, fell in love with cooking, and went all-in on AI.
