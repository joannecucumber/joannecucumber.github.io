Product Overview產品概覽

Xiaogao E-Invoice combines an electronic receipt carrier with character progression. Alongside linking a carrier, checking receipts, and displaying a barcode, users grow characters as receipts accumulate, build a collection, and change their carrier’s appearance.

I owned product proposals, features, UI/UX, and reusable IP-module specifications. The partner illustrator created the characters and artwork.

Business Context & Challenges商業背景與挑戰

CMoney already operated receipt-carrier products. Partnering with different IP creators offered a way to reach their audiences, increase carrier linking, and grow the user base and receipt volume to support B2B data marketing services.

Xiaogao was the first app in this partnership direction. It needed to motivate fans to download, link a carrier, and continue collecting receipts, while establishing a reusable foundation for subsequent products.

Character interactions therefore needed a clear connection to receipt collection. The collaboration model also needed to help illustrators, design, and engineering keep delivering new products efficiently.

Strategy & Execution策略與執行

Connect receipt collection to character progression

Character progression was tied to saving receipts, letting users unlock a collection and carrier appearances as receipts accumulated. Characters could respond to an everyday behavior, giving fans a continued sense of the IP’s identity within a simple progression loop.

Receipt lookup, barcode display, and settings retained their primary operating positions so character feedback could coexist with utility tasks. Progression used a lightweight mechanism, concentrating rewards around the existing act of collecting receipts and keeping routine carrier use direct.

Design the core features and journeys

Work with operations clarified the partnership direction and carrier-adoption goals. The proposal then became login, linking, home progression, collection, scanning, receipt details, appearance, and sharing flows. This connected IP interactions to the core product and made the required delivery scope visible to collaborators.

A small pre-launch usability check identified comprehension problems in carrier linking, and the flow was revised before development was complete. The design board documented final screens and states for implementation. That check informed a design correction; it was not used to claim that a particular change caused the subsequent linking rate.

Establish reusable IP modules

The fixed structure covered login, carrier linking, lookup, barcode display, and settings. Characters, illustrations, colors, selected buttons, and progression assets formed the replaceable layer. Later partnerships could begin with established functionality and replace IP content within a consistent core flow.

The module also allowed different progression modes so creators could choose a growth pattern suited to their characters. Expressions beyond the established boundaries could be discussed as module extensions. Clarifying what stayed fixed and what could change gave the team a shared starting point for the next partnership.

Define cross-team asset specifications

The specifications documented dimensions, placement, and reserved areas for home-screen components, buttons, character images, and progression stages. The specifications identified replaceable colors and assets, including growth, appearance, and angry states, so partner illustrators knew what they needed to supply.

The partner illustrators created the characters and artwork. Engineering handoff aligned asset boundaries, interfaces, and interaction states with implementation. The shared specifications helped collaborators establish what each screen needed, how assets mapped to states, and which requirements needed further discussion before handoff.

Outcomes & Impact成果與影響

74–81%Carrier-linking rate; comparable in-house apps were at 50–60%
3 → ~1 weekLaunch cycle for subsequent creator-partner receipt apps

The product was named Google Play’s 2022 Most Promising App and was featured on the App Store home page in January 2023.

Carrier linking and platform recognition are team product outcomes. The shorter launch cycle reflects the overall collaboration and delivery process. Supporting B2B data marketing was a business objective; no separately measured B2B impact is claimed here.
