Product Overview產品概覽

Laika turns real articles into material for reading, lookup, saved vocabulary, and practice. The same content supports independent learners at their own pace and helps teachers carry a lesson beyond the classroom.

My AI-builder approach connects problem definition and scope decisions with the reading interface, AI tasks, and cloud data, then checks the result in real usage states.

Explore: Reduce the obstacle of not knowing what to read

A URL field still requires people to find an article themselves. Explore provides a curated starting point for new users and reuses the existing translation flow, focusing the investment on getting from discovery to reading.

What to validateObserve the path from choosing an Explore article to reading and saving vocabulary. Establish whether people use that entry before investing in personalization or more complex AI recommendation. No conversion improvement is claimed so far.

Strategy & Execution策略與執行

Engineering: Connect reading tasks into a working service

A lightweight single-page web app separates extraction, structure analysis, translation batches, vocabulary, and speech. Original text becomes readable first, followed by translations; accounts, data, and content supply have distinct service boundaries.

AI collaboration: Move from generated functionality to integration checks

I define the problem, divide the implementation, and make scope decisions. AI-assisted code is checked within navigation, sign-in, synchronization, and cross-platform data updates.

Independent learners: Understand an article, then practice its unfamiliar words

Choose an interesting article and use translation, lookup, and audio as needed. Save unfamiliar words while reading, then return to flashcards, sentence exercises, or matching to practice active recall.

Design decisionKeep lookup beside the original text, preserve the source when saving, and use that same material for review. Each step reduces switching and repeated preparation.

Teachers: Capture the lesson as you teach, then hand it over to students

Add teaching points directly beside an article. Share the article and its notes after class so students can return to the same material, ask questions, and practice at their own pace.

Design decisionShare both the article and classroom notes. The teacher's context stays with the material while students continue with their own saved vocabulary and practice.
