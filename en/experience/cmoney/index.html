My product design work at CMoney

2016–2024

I was the sole designer on these products, except for Chip K-Line.

Product Timeline

  1. 2017–2018

    Modular design for investing apps

    I created the first design system for Chip K-Line, a stock charting app, and designed shared modules for apps built for investment educators.

    • Chip K-Line
    • Lin En-Ru
    • Chang’s Market Alerts
    • Daniel Investing
    • Futures Market

  2. 2018–2020

    Designing everyday tools

    Before Taiwan’s Ministry of Finance released its official app, I designed features to help people link their e-invoice carriers and scan barcodes. Data from the invoice products also supported advertising partnerships and new tools such as iCheck and Costco Deals.

    • Receipt Rewards
    • E-Invoice Carrier
    • iCheck
    • Costco Deals

  3. 2020–2024

    Building new products from scratch

    User research
    Product design

    I led the team in exploring product ideas through design thinking, user interviews, and Lean Canvas. I planned and designed the first versions and worked with engineering to launch creator collaborations like Banana Man E-Invoice, Xiaogao E-Invoice, and Mom & Dad Storytime, along with our own ideas such as Toast English.

    We worked in two-week cycles. In the first week, we finished the feature and screen designs and handed them off to engineering. The next week, we researched the next product idea.

    Research methods User interviews, surveys, competitive analysis, desk research, personas, and customer journey maps.

    • Xiaogao E-Invoice
    • Toast English
    • Mom & Dad Storytime
    • Cindy English
    • Banana Man E-Invoice
    • StockCast
    • Alarm Dating
