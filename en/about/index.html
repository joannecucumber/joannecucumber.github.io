About Me

Chieh-An (Joanne) Chien

Senior Product Designer / Design Lead

I'm a Senior Product Designer with 10 years of experience in UI/UX design, passionate about creative problem-solving, human-centered design, and design leadership. I hold a Master's degree in Design from National Taiwan University of Science and Technology (Taiwan Tech) and am dedicated to creating meaningful impact through design.

I've designed over 20 apps across diverse industries, tackling challenges ranging from 0-to-1 product launches and large-scale redesigns to lifestyle and productivity tools. Beyond hands-on design, I've led a design department of 30+ members, establishing design standards, mentoring talent, and driving AI adoption across workflows.

My expertise combines visual design, UX strategy, and user research (including interviews, surveys, and NPS analysis) to deliver measurable business outcomes and meaningful user experiences.
