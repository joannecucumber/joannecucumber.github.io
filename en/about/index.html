About Me

Chieh-An (Joanne) Chien

Head of Design Department | Product Designer

I'm Joanne, a product designer with nearly 10 years of experience. I'm Head of Design Department at CMoney in Taipei, leading a 30+ person design team and reshaping how it works with AI.

I've designed 20+ apps across fintech, lifestyle and productivity — from 0-to-1 launches to the sign-up, mission and subscription redesign of Forest, a focus app used worldwide. At CMoney, I designed the 0-to-1 e-invoice app (囂搞發票), named Google Play's 2022 Most Promising App.

Since 2025 I've led the design department, shifting the team's bar from polished UI to sound product judgment and building design standards, reviews and a competency framework. I also lead our AI x Design Ops program, cutting design workflow time by 60–90%, and built Moji, my own AI-made app, which reached #6 in Photo & Video on the App Store.

I turn fuzzy problems into clear product decisions through interviews, surveys and NPS, and help designers become real product partners.

I hold a Master's in Design from National Taiwan University of Science and Technology (2026). As first author, I published “From Static Profiles to Dynamic Conversations: AI Persona Construction and Human Comparison in Design Research” at HCII 2026 (Springer LNCS), a study on building conversational AI personas and comparing them with human participants in design research.
