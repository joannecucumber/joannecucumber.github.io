Chieh-An (Joanne) Chien

Senior Product Designer | Head of Design Department

I'm Joanne, a senior product designer focused on UI/UX and design leadership, with nearly 10 years of product design experience. I'm currently Head of Design Department at CMoney in Taipei.

I've designed 20+ apps across fintech, lifestyle, and productivity, from 0-to-1 launches to large-scale redesigns. At Seekrtech, on the global focus app Forest, I led the redesign of the registration flow, the mission system, and the subscription flow: significantly lifted sign-up conversion, gave users a reason to come back every day, and the subscription redesign drove revenue growth. At CMoney, I designed the 0-to-1 E-invoice app (囂搞發票), named Google Play's 2022 Most Promising App.

Today I lead a 40-person design department, setting design standards and review processes, mentoring designers, and driving AI adoption that cut workflow time by 60–90%.

My work combines visual design, UX strategy, and user research (interviews, surveys, NPS).

I hold a Master's in Design from National Taiwan University of Science and Technology (2026). As first author with Prof. Hsien-Hui Tang, I published “From Static Profiles to Dynamic Conversations: AI Persona Construction and Human Comparison in Design Research” at HCII 2026 (Springer LNCS), a study on building conversational AI personas and comparing them with human participants in design research.
